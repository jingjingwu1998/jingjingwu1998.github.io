About me

I am a Ph.D. student conducting cardiovascular research in Dr. Da-Zhi Wang's laboratory at the University of South Florida.

My research focuses on understanding how mitochondrial protein translation influences heart function, particularly in the context of obesity-related cardiovascular disease. I investigate how changes in this process affect the heart’s response to metabolic stress. By integrating preclinical disease models, cardiac functional assessments, molecular biology, and transcriptomic analyses, I aim to uncover underlying disease mechanisms and identify potential therapeutic targets for cardiometabolic diseases.

Beyond academic research, I have experience in pharmaceutical business development, commercial strategy, and biotechnology investment. I am also passionate about mentoring junior researchers and fostering interdisciplinary scientific collaboration.
