Research Skills

Research Skills

  • Preclinical Cardiovascular Research: Experimental design, HFpEF and HFrEF disease models, obesogenic feeding, myocardial infarction (MI), transverse aortic constriction (TAC), isoproterenol-induced cardiac stress, plasma collection, and subcutaneous (SC), intraperitoneal (IP), and intracardiac injections.
  • Cardiac and Metabolic Phenotyping: Echocardiography, cardiac MRI data analysis, treadmill exercise testing, DEXA, glucose tolerance testing, and circulating lipid quantification.
  • Histology and Imaging: Histological and immunostaining study design and image analysis (H&E, PSFG, IHC/IF), and transmission electron microscopy (TEM).
  • Mitochondrial Function: Seahorse extracellular flux analysis, citrate synthase activity assays, and mitochondrial Complex I enzymatic activity assays.
  • Molecular and Cellular Biology: CRISPR/Cas9, molecular cloning, mammalian cell culture, cell transfection, flow cytometry, RT-qPCR (miRNA and mRNA), and Western blotting.
  • Computational and Bioinformatics: RNA-seq, Hi-C, micro-CT image analysis, ImageJ, MeshLab, R, and Linux.
